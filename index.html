Smart Autonomous Irrigation System

Featured System

An automated irrigation system designed to reduce water wastage and manual intervention using multi-stage level sensing, fallback scheduling, and mechanical valve regulation.

Problem: Excessive manual effort and severe water wastage in traditional agriculture due to irregular scheduling and unmonitored pump cycles.
Solution: Engineered a closed-loop controller incorporating real-time water-level detection, pump control logic, safety timer fallback, and a four-bar mechanism.

Key Features:

  • Fail-safe timer fallback mechanism to prevent dry runs
  • Four-bar valve linkage for regulated fluid dispatch
  • Automated pump activation & shutoff control

IoT & Environmental

IoT Air Quality Monitoring System

Environmental IoT

A specialized multi-sensor IoT safety system engineered to continuously monitor ambient air toxicity, dangerous gases, temperature, and humidity across high-occupancy environments like hospitals, clinics, schools, and academic labs.

Problem: Lack of real-time air quality monitoring and gas hazard warnings in sensitive indoor public areas like classrooms, patient wards, and labs.
Solution: Engineered an ESP32-powered telemetry node integrating MQ2, MQ7, MQ135, and DHT11 sensors to capture and alert hazardous thresholds with immediate visual indicators.

Key Features:

  • Multi-gas detection covering CO, smoke, and air toxins (MQ2, MQ7, MQ135)
  • Calibrated for indoor safety in schools, hospitals & campuses
  • Real-time temperature & humidity logging via DHT11
  • Continuous sensor telemetry processed on ESP32 microcontroller

Piezoelectric Power Generation Footwear

Green Tech

A sustainable wearable prototype that harvests kinetic energy while walking, converting mechanical pressure into usable electrical power and displaying real-time power generation data.

Problem: Wasted kinetic energy during daily foot travel and lack of wearable off-grid renewable energy harvesting for portable electronics.
