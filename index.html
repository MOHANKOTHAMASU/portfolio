How It Works (Piezoelectric Harvesting)

Kinetic Compression

Foot strike applies mechanical pressure across piezoelectric ceramic discs.

2
AC Spike Generation

Transducers convert dynamic stress into high-voltage, low-current AC electrical pulses.

3
Bridge Rectification

Diode bridge converts AC spikes into DC charge and stores it in smoothing capacitor.

4
Microcontroller Telemetry

Arduino UNO ADC tracks accumulated potential and renders real-time energy output on LCD.
